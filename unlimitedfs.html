Spin 3 Engagement Did Not Convert Into Promotion Claims

Spin 3 recorded the highest wheel participation in Brazil, with 166 players spinning the ticket. However, only six players opted in and no player successfully claimed the full 7 Days Unlimited Free Spins promotion.

1

Wheel Engagement Is Moderate

58.4% of recipients spun at least one wheel. There is still an opportunity to re-engage the 122 players who did not use any of their available tickets.

2

Spin 3 Attracts the Most Interest

The 7 Days Unlimited Free Spins ticket recorded the highest spin rate at 57%, outperforming Spin 1 and Spin 2.

3

The Main Drop Happens After the Spin

Players are willing to spin, but very few continue to opt in, deposit and complete the promotional journey.

High Wheel Participation, but No Completed Promotion Claims

Mexico recorded strong wheel engagement, with 1,039 players using Spin 3. Despite this high level of interaction, only 11 players opted in and no player successfully claimed the full 7 Days Unlimited Free Spins promotion.

1

Wheel Engagement Is Strong

81.05% of recipients spun at least one wheel, showing that the Lucky Wheel mechanic is effective at capturing the attention of newly registered players.

2

Spin 3 Is the Strongest Ticket

Spin 3 achieved an 80.4% spin rate, making it the most used ticket and the most attractive reward concept in the campaign.

3

Post-Spin Conversion Is the Bottleneck

The largest decline happens after the player sees the reward. The additional opt-in and deposit steps are not converting the majority of interested players.

Mexico Summary

Mexico recorded strong wheel engagement and a higher overall post-spin deposit rate than Brazil. However, none of the players completed a claim for the 7 Days Unlimited Free Spins promotion. This shows that the wheel is successfully attracting attention, but the promotion structure or activation journey is not converting that attention into completed participation.
