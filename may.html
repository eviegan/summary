Rewards Lobby Performance Summary

Summary of player login behaviour, Weekly Cashback claim performance, and Daily Rakeback redemption after moving rewards into the Rewards Lobby.

Executive Summary

Before the Rewards Lobby launch, Weekly Cashback was automatically credited into player wallets at 8:00 PM local time. Since players did not need to manually claim the reward, there was no meaningful claim-rate data before launch.

After the Rewards Lobby launch, Weekly Cashback became available at 10:00 AM local time. Players now need to manually claim the reward before expiry. This created a measurable claim journey.

Daily Rakeback was also included in Rewards Lobby starting from 29/04/2026. Players receive rakeback based on same-day gameplay at 23:59 local time, and must redeem it within 3 days.

Important comparison rule: Before Rewards Lobby, use login data only because cashback was auto-credited. After Rewards Lobby, we can review both login data and claim/redemption behaviour.

Daily Rakeback Mechanism

Daily Rakeback is generated based on players’ gameplay activity on the same day. The reward is issued at 23:59 and becomes available in the Rewards Lobby for manual redemption.

⏳

Expiry Creates Urgency

Since the reward expires within 3 days, players are encouraged to return frequently to check and redeem their rakeback before it expires.

📊

BR vs MX Daily Rakeback Performance

BR achieved a claim rate of 64.95%, while MX achieved 63.13%. Both markets showed similar redemption behaviour, with BR slightly higher by 1.82 percentage points.

Summary: Daily Rakeback adds another recurring reason for players to visit Rewards Lobby. Unlike Weekly Cashback, which is tied to a weekly cycle, Daily Rakeback creates daily reward visibility and encourages players to return more frequently.

Overall Findings

⏰

Weekly Cashback timing changed from 8PM to 10AM

Before launch, players received cashback automatically at 8PM. After launch, rewards became available at 10AM and required manual claiming before expiry.

📊

Pre-launch claim rate cannot be compared

Since cashback was auto-credited before launch, there was no manual claim action. Login activity is the correct pre-launch engagement indicator.

🎁

Rewards Lobby created measurable reward behaviour

Both Weekly Cashback and Daily Rakeback now require player action, giving clearer data on entitlement, claim, and redemption performance.

🇧🇷

BR showed stronger post-launch login activity

BR maintained strong 8PM traffic after launch, with 38 and 39 logins, while 10AM became the new Weekly Cashback claim-start window.

🇲🇽

MX remained more stable but less aggressive

MX 8PM login was stable before launch at 26 logins, reached 27 on launch week, then reduced to 22 in the second week.

Final Conclusion

Rewards Lobby created a stronger and measurable reward journey

The main impact of Rewards Lobby is the shift from passive reward delivery to active player participation. Weekly Cashback changed from auto-credit at 8PM into manual claim from 10AM, while Daily Rakeback creates daily return motivation through 23:59 reward issuance and 3-day expiry.
