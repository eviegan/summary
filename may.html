The purpose is to monitor engagement quality, identify drop-off stages, and evaluate whether the emotional journey supports deposit conversion.

Final Presentation Summary

1

The funnel structure is useful for monitoring

Because all percentages are based on entitled players, the report clearly shows where players continue and where they drop off.

2

The emotional thank-you step appears workable

Both BR and Mexico maintain strong 2nd-ticket reach, meaning the thank-you stage helps keep players engaged before the deposit offer.

3

The final deposit stage needs enhancement

The biggest drop-off happens after players receive the 3rd ticket. Next testing should focus on stronger CTA wording, clearer reward value, countdown urgency, or follow-up reminders.

Overall: Mexico is currently performing better than BR in both engagement and conversion. However, both markets show the same pattern: the ticket journey can drive engagement, but the final deposit conversion requires further optimization.
