Daily Rakeback
FastTrack Reward Summary

New Daily Rakeback is issued through FastTrack Reward and fired to player lobby. Players have 3 days to manually claim the reward.

Launch Date: 29/04/2026

Key Takeaway

After changing from automatic rakeback crediting to a manual FastTrack claim flow, the claim rate is lower than the previous login rate. This is expected because the new journey adds extra actions: login, enter lobby, notice reward, and claim within 3 days.

Key Insights

Presentation summary
1
Claim rate is lower than previous login rate

This is because the new reward flow requires manual player action instead of automatic crediting.

2
3-day expiry creates urgency but also drop-off

Players who miss the lobby reward or delay their login may lose the chance to claim.

3
Login intent remains strong

Previous login benchmark shows players were active, so the main issue is likely reward visibility and claim friction.

4
Optimization opportunity

Improve lobby visibility, add reminders before expiry, and track lobby view to claim conversion.

Recommended Next Steps

How to improve the new Daily Rakeback claim journey
✓
Enhance reward visibility in lobby

Make the Daily Rakeback reward easier to notice with stronger CTA, icon, or highlighted reward card.

✓
Add reminder communication before expiry

Send reminder notification or inbox message before the 3-day claim window ends.

✓
Track full funnel metrics

Track Entitled → Login → Lobby Visit → Reward View → Claim to identify the exact drop-off stage.
