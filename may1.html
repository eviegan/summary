Executive Summary

1. FastTrack Created Additional Claim Friction

Previously, Daily Rakeback was credited automatically. After FastTrack launch, players must login, see the reward in lobby, and manually claim it within 3 days. Therefore, claim rate is naturally lower than previous login rate.

2. Expiry Is Mainly Driven By One-Time Deposit Players

BR shows 81.45% and MX shows 90.23% of unique expired players deposited only once. This indicates the expired reward issue is strongly linked to early churn and weak revisit behavior.

3. Recommended Action

Improve reward visibility in lobby, add expiry reminder communication, and build a separate reactivation journey for new / one-time deposit players before the 3-day expiry ends.
