Executive Summary

From 12–22 June, the Brazil Daily Mission generated 474 deposit events. A total of 253 completions were recorded at Tier 1, 110 at Tier 2 and 13 at Tier 3. On average, each day had 43 deposit players, 23 Tier 1 completions, 10 Tier 2 completions and 1 Tier 3 completion.

The R$100 milestone is the main engagement driver. The R$500 milestone helps push higher deposit behaviour, while R$3,000 remains suitable as a premium/VIP target. Token usage is strong, but expired tokens should be reduced through reminder messages.
